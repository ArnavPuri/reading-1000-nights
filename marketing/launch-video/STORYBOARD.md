# 1000 Nights — launch film storyboard

**Positioning line:** *A thousand nights. One story, one poem, one essay.*
**Cuts:** 9:16 hero (1080×1920, 39.5 s) · 16:9 restage (1920×1080, 39.5 s) · 1:1 looping teaser (1080×1080, 7 s)
**Frame rate:** 30 fps · **Audio:** synthesised ambient bed (see §6); every cut also works muted

---

## 1. Concept: "One candle, every night"

The film is a single evening, played as a ritual. It begins in darkness, as the app's own night does. An ember
**breathes** at a wick and a match-strike throws a few sparks. The candle from the app icon catches, and its light
**spreads outward** over the engraved book until the icon is fully lit. That candle is the thread through the film:
it lights the title, it lights again inside the app when a night is finished, and it lights a third time on the end
card. The motif teaches the product's reward loop (read, then light a candle) without a line of explanation.

Between the candles we show what you actually get: three 19th-century engraving plates, each paired with a real
line from the pack (a story, a poem, an essay), then the real app screens in a phone. The film moves the way the
app moves, slowly and sparsely, with cross-dissolves, light that blooms and settles, and stars lighting one at a
time. Nothing bounces.

Tone references: a match struck in a quiet room; the 3 a.m. stillness of an old library; a book of engravings
whose plates you turn by lamplight.

## 3. Animation principles, shot by shot

| Principle | Where and how |
|---|---|
| **Timing and spacing** | Big gestures are slow and small ones are quick. Light spreading takes 2.4 s, a dissolve 0.85–0.9 s, a UI band settle 1.0 s, a star catching 0.5 s, a letter resolving 1.0 s with 50 ms stagger. Spacing is front-loaded (ease-out) for arrivals and back-loaded (ease-in) for departures. |
| **Slow in / slow out** | Six named curves (§4). Dissolves use a symmetric sine-like `cubic-bezier(.45,0,.55,1)`. Arrivals use cubic-out `(.33,1,.68,1)`. Light uses expo-out `(.16,1,.3,1)` so the flame catches fast and settles long. Departures use ease-in `(.5,0,.75,0)`. Camera and object moves use `(.65,0,.35,1)`. |
| **Anticipation** | Shot 1: the ember "breathes" for 0.6 s on a slow ease-in curve before the strike. Shot 4c: the same 0.45 s ember breath precedes the in-app candle. Shot 5: a third strike. The audience learns the beat and leans into it. |
| **Staging** | One idea per beat, one focal point per frame. The candle is centred alone before any type appears. Plates are dimmed into navy with feathered edges so the quote is the only bright type. The phone's screen is the brightest object in shots 4a–d, and the caption sits in its own zone. In 16:9 the plates alternate left/right so the eye zig-zags rather than sitting on one side. |
| **Follow-through and overlapping action** | When the candle glides up in shot 2, the flame skews against the motion and compresses, driven by the candle's velocity delayed by 120 ms, then recovers after the move ends. Letters and words have 1.0 s durations but only 50–160 ms staggers, so several are always mid-flight. The three nouns of the ladder trail their "one" by 160 ms. UI bands overlap by 60–160 ms. The share card's rotation settles 350 ms after its position does. Each plate's quote starts 450 ms after its image and leaves 400 ms before it. |
| **Secondary action** | Candle flicker: three octaves of seeded value-noise drive flame height, width, skew and glow. Embers rise on sine-wave arcs, cooling from gold to red as they fade. Dust motes drift with parallax. Stars light and twinkle. The reader's ribbon progresses while the text scrolls. Week-dots light in sequence. A soft bloom accompanies each constellation star. |
| **Arcs** | Sparks follow true ballistic parabolas. Embers rise on sinusoidal arcs. Ken Burns focus points travel along quadratic Bézier curves, not straight lines: down-and-across for the coins, up for the stars, up the winding road for El Dorado. |
| **Appeal** | Everything is lit by one warm source. The phone has a faint amber rim-light on the candle side. Fine film grain (7 % overlay, refreshed at 15 fps) gives a printed, engraved texture and dithers the dark gradients against banding. The type is set in the app's own Newsreader. |
| **Squash and stretch: deliberately avoided** | Nothing deforms or bounces. The brand is calm and still. The only "stretch" is physical: the flame lengthens slightly when the candle moves, which reads as drag, not cartoon elasticity. UI settles use a quint-out with at most a 1.2 % overshoot, so they land rather than bounce. The ignition has a 6 % brightness flare that relaxes, which is light behaviour, not motion. |
| **Solid drawing / exaggeration** | Kept minimal. All imagery is the product's own engravings and real screenshots, so we exaggerate only light, never form. |

## 4. Motion spec

| Token | Curve | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `dissolve` | cubic-bezier(.45, 0, .55, 1) | Cross-dissolves, opacity |
| `bloom` | cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1) | Flame catching, star catching |
| `light` | cubic-bezier(.30, .55, .40, 1) | Candle light spreading over the icon |
| `out` | cubic-bezier(.33, 1, .68, 1) | Type and caption arrivals (blur 14→0 px, rise 22→0 px) |
| `settle` | cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1.012) | Phone, UI bands, share card (≈1.2 % max overshoot) |
| `move` | cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1) | Candle reposition, reader scroll, ribbon, constellation lines |
| `anticip` | cubic-bezier(.55, 0, .9, .35) | Ember "breath" before each strike |
| `exit` | cubic-bezier(.5, 0, .75, 0) | Departures (fade + 14–30 px lift) |
| `drift` | cubic-bezier(.3, .1, .7, .9) | Ken Burns zoom/pan, slow push-ins |

Durations: dissolves 0.45 s (in-app, matching the app's own cross-fade) and 0.85–0.9 s (film plates). Type reveal is
1.0 s per letter with 50 ms stagger, or 0.9 s per word with 70–160 ms stagger. Ken Burns runs the full 4.2–4.8 s
plate duration with zoom 1.00↔1.10.

## 6. Sound: cue sheet

The bed is synthesised in `tools/synth_audio.py` (numpy only: sines, bell partials and FFT-shaped noise, seeded, so it
is deterministic). It uses **no samples and no third-party audio**. Cue times are read from `window.CUES` in
`src/comp.js` at render time, so sound is locked to picture. It is mastered very quietly (about −26 LUFS integrated, peaks around −11 dBFS)
because it is meant to be felt, and the piece works muted.

| Time (s) | Cue | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 → | Room tone | Low-passed brown-ish noise, barely there |
| 1.27 | Match strike | 90 ms of 2–7 kHz noise and a few crackles |
| 1.36 | Flame catches | Soft low "whoomph" (90–900 Hz noise swell, 0.45 s decay) |
| 1.4 → 39.5 | Drone pad | Open D (D2 A2 D3 A3 E4), detuned sine pairs, swelling in over 3 s, breathing at 0.07 Hz, lifting slightly during the app section |
| 4.05 | Title | Bell on D5 |
| 9.3 / 12.75 / 16.2 | Plates | Airy page-turn swell |
| 20.75 | Phone arrives | Bell on A5 |
| 26.7 | In-app candle | Smaller whoomph |
| 29.55 → 31.35 | Journey stars | Seven glints rising on D-major pentatonic, panned left→right like the constellation |
| 34.95 | End-card strike | Whoomph |
| 35.45 | Wordmark | Bell on A4 |
| 39.0 → 39.5 | Fade | Everything fades to silence with the picture |

To ship silent, remux with `ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -c copy -an out.mp4`.

## 7. Production notes

- The source is `src/comp.js`, a single canvas composition. `window.renderFrame(t)` is a pure function of `t`.
  There is no wall clock, and all randomness comes from seeded `mulberry32`. `render.mjs` steps `t = frame/30`.
- `tools/prep_assets.py` derives the "unlit" states (icon without flame plus a flame sprite; unlit in-app candle;
  grey week-dots; dimmed constellation; reader without ribbon) from the real assets. Lighting is then animated by
  compositing the original pixels back additively, so every lit frame is the real artwork.
- The reader scroll continues past the bottom of the real screenshot using the story's actual text from
  `pack-001.json`, set in Newsreader 54/90 px at the app's exact measure, so the seam cannot be seen.
